WHAT THIS MEANS MECHANISTICALLY
The 4-epoch result was +4.47x noise; at 20 epochs the same model is
-0.10x on mAP50 and -0.38x on mAP50_95. The gain did not shrink -
it vanished. That is the signature of a CONVERGENCE-SPEED effect, not a
capacity effect: the deeper reconstruction reaches the same place sooner,
and the 1x1 baseline catches up by epoch 20. Every cell in this arm is
statistically indistinguishable at convergence.

This is the second time a 4-epoch reading has failed to survive 20
epochs in this project (probe A: detection control +0.26x at 4ep,
-2.53x at 20ep). 4-epoch probes are not usable for architecture
decisions here, only for cheap falsification of large predicted effects.

Standing result from probe B that is NOT affected: Q2's sign reversal at
4 epochs was large (3.64x) but the 20-epoch dilated cell is still the
lowest of the three on mAP50 (-0.95x vs baseline). Dilation is not
rescued by convergence; it is simply not resolvable at this noise level.
