All three probes point the same way: the segmentation tasks are not
limited by how much of Z they get (A), nor by how hard they push on it
(C). Detection is limited by the reconstruction, and specifically by its
depth rather than its receptive field (B). The two groups of tasks have
different bottlenecks, which is the case for a task-aware FINAL design
but not for a task-aware SHARED Z.
