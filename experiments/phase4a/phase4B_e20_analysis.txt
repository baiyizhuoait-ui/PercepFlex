Verdict
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A = NO, confirmed at 20 epochs, and now for a stronger reason than at 4ep.
  Per-task projection buys lane +1.16x noise (unresolved) and costs detection
  -1.68x on mAP50 and -2.53x on mAP50_95 (both beyond the 2x threshold). The
  4ep null was therefore not a power artefact: extending the cell did not
  rescue the prediction, it revealed a cost that 4ep was hiding.

  New observation, and the reason this is not merely a bigger null: a change
  confined to the lane branch moved detection by more than 2x noise. The three
  tasks are strongly coupled through Z. That is independent evidence for the
  premise of probe C (gradient composition on Z is load-bearing), and it means
  probe A should not be read as 'the tasks are independent'.
