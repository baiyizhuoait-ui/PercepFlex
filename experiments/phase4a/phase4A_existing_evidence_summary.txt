### 2. THE R0 STRUCTURAL LIMITATION (why Phase 4A exists)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  R0:   Encoder -> Detection
        Encoder -> Z -> DA
        Encoder -> Z -> Lane

  Detection never reads Z. So an observed 'Z does not matter for detection'
  is consistent with AT LEAST these distinct explanations, which R0 cannot separate:

    A  detection is genuinely saturated at z=16
    B  detection bypasses Z, so the experiment never tested it   <-- R0 artifact
    C  the encoder, not Z, is the binding constraint
    D  the Z -> detection reconstruction is too weak
    E  Z carries too little spatial information (1/8, rebuilt from F2/F3/F4)
    F  task gradients conflict and suppress Z utilisation
    G  one shared Z is simply the wrong interface for these three tasks

  R2 removes explanation B by construction. The rest are still open and are
  what the diagnostics in STEP 5 are for.
