Established facts carried into Phase 5 as PRIOR (do not re-derive):
  * In R0 the detection head bypasses Z entirely: ||dL_det/dZ|| is exactly
    0.0000 at every width. R0's 'Z does not matter for detection' is an
    artefact of that bypass, not a property of Z.
  * Forcing detection through Z (R2) flips the SIGN of its Z-sensitivity:
    -0.0043 mAP50 in R0 (0.59x noise) becomes +0.0171 (2.34x noise) in R2.
  * Effective rank of Z grows SUB-linearly: 42.5% of channels used at z16,
    37.1% at z32, 24.9% at z128, with zero dead channels. Widening Z buys
    correlated channels, not independent ones.

F. ALREADY EXCLUDED -- treat as PRIOR, do not repeat
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Wider shared Z (Phase 3B/3C). z16->z128 buys lane +2.25x noise and DA
     nothing, at +93% FLOPs. Effective rank falls from 42.5% to 24.9%.
  2. Per-task projection width (probe A, 4ep AND 20ep). Lane gain 0.25x
     noise at 4ep, 1.16x at 20ep, while detection LOST 2.53x noise even
     though only the lane branch was touched -- so the tasks are coupled
     through Z. Verdict: NO.
  3. Gradient rebalancing (probe C, 20ep, two widths). lambda_det=0.2
     passed its manipulation check (weighted det share ~0.75 -> 0.33/0.47)
     and cost -13.07x noise mAP50 for +0.27x noise DA. Verdict: REJECTED.
  4. Dilated reconstruction (probe B). Dilation was worse than equal-
     parameter plain depth by 3.64x noise. Verdict: NO.
  5. Reconstruction depth (20ep confirmation). The +4.47x noise 4ep gain
     VANISHED at 20ep (-0.10x / -0.38x). Verdict: 4-EPOCH ARTEFACT.

  Methodological consequence, now a standing rule: 4-epoch readings have
  twice failed to survive 20 epochs in this project (probe A's detection
  control went from +0.26x at 4ep to -2.53x at 20ep). A 4-epoch probe may
  be used to FALSIFY a large predicted effect; it may not be used to
  ESTABLISH one.
