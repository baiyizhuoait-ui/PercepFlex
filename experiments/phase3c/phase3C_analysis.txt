### F. FIXED-BUDGET DOMINANCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Only pairs whose parameter gap is within 5% are tested at all. Inside such a
  pair the two cells are treated as having the SAME parameter budget by construction,
  so a 1-2% residual gap is neither an advantage nor a disadvantage.

  TIER 1 (strict)      params <=, FLOPs <=, every metric >= raw value.
  TIER 2 (noise-aware) same budget, FLOPs <=, no metric worse by more than the
                       noise floor, at least one metric better beyond the noise floor.

  Tier 2 is the decision rule that applies here. Tier 1 is reported only for
  transparency: it fails on this data purely because of sub-noise and sub-tolerance
  residuals, not because any allocation is genuinely competitive.
