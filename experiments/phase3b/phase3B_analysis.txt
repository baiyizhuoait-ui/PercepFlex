### E. DA / LANE BOTTLENECK DIAGNOSIS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 3A saw DA/Lane flatten from E-base to E-large at z=16. Two rival
explanations: (A) the tasks are genuinely saturated, or (B) z=16 is
throttling the richer encoder features before they reach the seg heads.
The discriminator is whether DA/Lane REVIVE when Z widens at E-large.

  metric           z16       z32      z128   d(32-16)  d(128-16)  verdict
  ------------------------------------------------------------------
  da_mIoU       0.8589    0.8546    0.8575    -0.0043    -0.0014  flat -> task saturation
  da_fg         0.7761    0.7699    0.7740    -0.0062    -0.0021  flat -> task saturation
  lane_mIoU     0.5878    0.5893    0.5925    +0.0015    +0.0047  REVIVES -> z bottleneck
  lane_fg       0.2000    0.2026    0.2085    +0.0026    +0.0085  REVIVES -> z bottleneck

  NOTE: even the 'task saturation' branch is only EVIDENCE CONSISTENT
  WITH saturation at z<=128 / 20 epochs. It is not a proof, and it
  does not rule out saturation at some larger Z we did not test.

### F. DETECTION DIAGNOSIS   (R0 bypasses Z)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In R0 the detection head reads encoder F2/F3/F4 directly; it never sees
Z. So ANY mAP response to z cannot be a direct Z effect -- it can only
be multi-task gradient coupling / shared training dynamics / indirect
effects through the shared encoder. Detection's primary driver must be
the ENCODER. Verify both claims below.

  mAP50: encoder main effect (z=16)  small 0.2687 -> base 0.3204 -> large 0.3585   total +0.0898
  mAP50: z effect at E-large            z16 0.3585 / z32 0.3494 / z128 0.3436   spread 0.0149  (noise 0.0073)
          -> exceeds noise: indirect/coupling effect, NOT direct Z consumption
  mAP50_95: encoder main effect (z=16)  small 0.0890 -> base 0.1149 -> large 0.1356   total +0.0466
  mAP50_95: z effect at E-large            z16 0.1356 / z32 0.1308 / z128 0.1271   spread 0.0085  (noise 0.0032)
          -> exceeds noise: indirect/coupling effect, NOT direct Z consumption

  Do NOT write 'detection consumes the compact representation' anywhere
  in the R0 report. That statement only becomes available under R2.
